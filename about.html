About Me

Learn more about who I am, what drives me, and where I come from.

Berthrand Kevin

Creative Technologist & UX Designer

I'm a multidisciplinary creative based in Cape Town, South Africa. With a background in both design and front-end development, I bridge the gap between aesthetics and functionality. My work focuses on creating meaningful digital experiences that are not only visually appealing but also intuitive and accessible.

I believe that great design has the power to solve real problems and make people's lives better. Whether it's a mobile app, a website, or a brand identity, I pour my passion into every project I touch.

🎯 What drives me

I'm constantly curious about how people interact with technology. My goal is to create work that feels human, thoughtful, and delightful.

Where I Call Home

Cape Town isn't just where I live—it's where I find endless inspiration. The vibrant street art in the city center, the creative energy of the Woodstock neighborhood, and the breathtaking views from Signal Hill all fuel my creative process. I love working from local coffee shops, collaborating with other creatives in the area, and contributing to South Africa's growing tech scene.

🌟 Fun fact: I once designed a mobile app for a local surf school while watching the sunset from Camp's Bay beach.
